## Student Information Dashboard
## Project Overview

This project develops an academic dashboard to visualize student demographic information for the Information Technology Department. Multiple institutional datasets were integrated to provide interactive insights into student distribution, enrollment characteristics, funding sources, geographic origins, and parental occupations.

<br>The dashboard supports evidence-based academic planning and administrative decision-making.

## Executive Summary

Student records from multiple institutional sources were standardized and consolidated into a centralized reporting system covering more than 1,800 students. Interactive visualizations reveal demographic trends, enrollment pathways, funding patterns, and regional distributions.

<br>The dashboard enables academic administrators to better understand student characteristics and supports planning for admissions, scholarship allocation, and international program development.

## Student Achievement Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project visualizes student achievement data to monitor competition participation and academic accomplishments across multiple years. The dashboard provides comprehensive insights into achievement trends, competition levels, award categories, and participating students.

<br>The solution supports continuous evaluation of student development programs within the department.

## Executive Summary

Achievement records from 2020 to 2023 were consolidated into an interactive dashboard that enables users to monitor student performance across local, national, and international competitions. The analysis identified peak achievement periods and revealed that the majority of awards were earned at the national level.

<br>These insights support academic departments in evaluating student development initiatives and planning future competition programs.
