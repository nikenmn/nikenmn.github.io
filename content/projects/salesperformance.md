## Project Overview

This project develops an interactive sales dashboard to monitor revenue, profit, product performance, customer purchasing behavior, and regional sales trends. The dashboard provides business stakeholders with an integrated view of overall sales performance while highlighting key revenue drivers.

<br>Interactive filtering enables faster exploration of sales trends across products, departments, and geographic regions.

## Executive Summary

Sales transaction data were transformed into an interactive dashboard featuring revenue, profit, regional performance, product contribution, and customer purchasing metrics. The analysis identified August as the strongest sales period, with East Java contributing the largest share of revenue, while lubricant and accessory products generated the highest profitability.

<br>The dashboard supports data-driven sales planning, inventory allocation, and strategic business decision-making by providing timely insights into operational performance.
