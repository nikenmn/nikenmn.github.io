## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn within the banking industry to identify customer segments with the highest likelihood of leaving the company. Using customer demographic and financial information, the project develops a risk-based segmentation framework that enables businesses to prioritize retention initiatives more effectively.

<br>The analysis transforms raw customer data into actionable business insights through interactive dashboards and customer risk categorization.

## Executive Summary

The bank experienced an overall customer churn rate of 20.37%, with more than two thousand customers leaving the service. Analysis of customer characteristics revealed that age was the strongest predictor of churn, while account balance significantly amplified churn risk among older customers.

<br>The highest-risk customer profile consisted of older customers with high account balances and low credit scores, exhibiting churn rates exceeding 58%. A structured High, Intermediate, and Low Risk segmentation framework was developed to help prioritize retention efforts, optimize resource allocation, and support proactive customer relationship management.
