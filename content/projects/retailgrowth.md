## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail transaction data to identify why business revenue remained stagnant despite consistent customer purchasing activity. Instead of focusing on customer acquisition, the analysis investigates customer spending behavior, transaction value, and customer segmentation to uncover opportunities for sustainable revenue growth.

<br>The project combines statistical analysis, customer segmentation, and business storytelling to identify the structural causes of revenue stagnation and provide actionable strategies for increasing customer value.

## Executive Summary

Although customer demand remained stable between 2022 and 2025, monthly revenue showed little long-term growth. The analysis revealed that revenue stagnation was primarily caused by low customer value distribution rather than insufficient demand. Approximately 87% of customers belonged to the Low and Medium spending segments, while the average basket size remained only around 5.5 items per transaction.

<br>Through customer segmentation and transaction behavior analysis, two strategic growth opportunities were identified: increasing basket size through improved cross-selling and product recommendations and encouraging customers to move into higher spending segments through targeted strategies. Scenario simulations demonstrated that relatively small improvements in transaction value could generate meaningful revenue growth without relying on additional customer acquisition.
