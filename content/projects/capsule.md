## Project Overview

This project focuses on developing an interactive production monitoring dashboard for a supplement manufacturing facility. The dashboard provides real-time visibility into production progress, raw material consumption, inventory levels, and future material requirements to improve operational transparency and production planning. The dashboard automates manual reporting processes while supporting supply chain decision-making through dynamic visualizations.

## 📈 Executive Summary

Production, inventory, and raw material data were integrated into a centralized dashboard to monitor manufacturing performance across multiple production stages. Interactive visualizations allow stakeholders to compare production plans with actual output, monitor inventory balances, and forecast future material requirements.

<br>The dashboard improves reporting efficiency, enables early identification of potential material shortages, and supports more effective production scheduling and inventory management.
